## DAM image-set manager (PDP) — how to demo

- Open the page containing the **F&D Product Detail** block (Venato, SKU 100610781) in the CMS **edit/preview** view (`/preview?...&ctx=edit`). The "Image set manager" card appears under the thumbnails; it is hidden on the public site.
- Reorder with the arrows or drag cards, add a new-arrival image URL ("Add as #1"), or remove one. The hero updates immediately; the change is saved and **published** to the shared FdProduct (so the grid and shop-the-look show it too).
- Reload the public PDP to show the new order. The status line shows the real save + publish time.
- Talk track: say "content edit, no purge ticket"; renditions and version-hashed URLs are DAM behaviour. The seed images are plain Unsplash URLs, so don't claim measured CDN timings.
- In the CMS **edit** iframe, clicks on the card are captured by the edit overlay. Use the floating **Open image manager in new tab** link (top right of the preview) and reorder there.

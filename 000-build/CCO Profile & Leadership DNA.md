## 2. What They Do (The Product Ecosystem)

* **Flagship Conversational AI — *Hygia™*:** An on-demand AI coach powered by **Motivational Interviewing (MI)**. Rather than diagnosing conditions or issuing generic wellness checklists, *Hygia* uses reflective listening, open-ended inquiry, and ambivalence resolution to help employees build intrinsic readiness for behavioral change (stress, sleep, nutrition, chronic illness management).
  * *Early Validation Study (2025):* 94% felt heard and understood, 90% identified a goal and were ready to act, 82% felt safe sharing personal thoughts, and 94% wanted to continue using it.
* **Predictive Modeling Engine — *HealthSignals™*:** Proprietary predictive risk stratification that isolates high-cost comorbidity overlap—specifically targeting the **23% of employees with both chronic and mental health conditions who drive >60% of employer healthcare costs**.
* **Employer Co-Branded Web Experience:** Dedicated employer landing pages and portals that co-brand with the employer or broker and adapt dynamically (e.g., starting with male/female communication styles and moving to deep clinical/lifestyle personas) to hit their aggressive **40–60% outreach engagement benchmark**.
* **Employer Intelligence Dashboard:** Aggregated, HIPAA-compliant reporting giving HR and benefits leaders visibility into workforce health sentiment, engagement rates, and projected healthcare cost savings.

## 3. Business Model & Monetization Mechanics

CCO operates a **B2B2C (Employer-Sponsored Health Benefit) SaaS & Data Insights Model**:

```
[CCO] 
  └── Sold via Benefit Brokers & Consultants (Mercer, WTW, Gallagher)
        └── Contracted by Enterprise Employers (Purchasers / Payers)
              └── Delivered Free & Confidentially to Employees (End Users)
```

### A. Channel-Led Distribution (B2B)
* CCO does not acquire consumers directly. They sell to **HR, Chief People Officers, and VP of Total Rewards** primarily through **employee benefit brokers, health plan consultants, and TPA intermediaries**.
* Brokers package CCO as a modern, high-engagement replacement or overlay to broken legacy EAPs (which typically see miserable 3–5% utilization).

### B. Monetization & Pricing Structures
* **PEPM (Per Employee Per Month) Subscription:** Standard corporate wellness pricing ($1.50 – $4.00+ PEPM across all eligible employees).
* **Value-Shared / Engaged User Pilot:** For early pilot employers (the 2–3 companies with 100–600 employees you discussed), they will likely deploy a **fixed pilot setup fee + engaged-user SaaS fee**, allowing them to prove the 40–60% engagement metric and clinical UX validation before signing multi-year enterprise contracts.
* **High Gross Margins (No Human Coach Bottleneck):** Because *Hygia™* is an AI-first coach rather than a telehealth network of live human therapists (like Lyra or Spring Health), their cost of goods sold (COGS) does not scale linearly with user volume, yielding SaaS gross margins (75–85%).

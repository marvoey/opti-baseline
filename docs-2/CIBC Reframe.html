Challenger Reframe

CIBC Mellon

Shifting the narrative from a technical modernization project to solving a critical client-intent gap.

The Stated Problem (Symptoms)

"We need better workflows, DAM, accessibility, analytics, governance, AI, and easier content management."

CIBC Mellon believes the central challenge is an outdated, hard-to-manage website. But a faster publishing process or new visual design doesn't solve the core issue—it just allows them to produce destination-based content more efficiently.

The Real Business Risk

CIBC Mellon is delivering digital experiences around predefined destinations, while its audience expects to express a need and receive a relevant answer.

Visitors must currently understand CIBC Mellon’s complex organizational structure, choose service categories, interpret terminology, and assemble the answers themselves.

Commercial Insight

The risk is not that CIBC Mellon cannot publish content efficiently. The risk is that its most valuable expertise remains difficult for clients and prospects to access at the exact moment they need it.

The Gap

The website is structured around what CIBC Mellon offers, while visitors arrive thinking about what they need to accomplish.

The Cost of Inaction

  • Relevant services remain undiscovered.
  • Thought leadership fails to influence decisions.
  • Visitors leave without recognizing capabilities.
  • Digital engagement yields activity without progression.
  • Competitors appear easier to work with.
The Provocative Question
"What happens if CIBC Mellon modernizes the platform, improves workflows, and migrates all of its content—but visitors still have to know where to look before they can find the right answer?"

This separates the technical modernization project from the true business outcome.

The Opportunity: A Paradigm Shift

Destination-Driven
“Here are our services. Navigate to the one you need.”
Intent-Driven
“Tell us what you are trying to accomplish, and we will assemble the relevant services, expertise, evidence, and next step.”



Optimizely is not simply helping CIBC Mellon manage and publish content more efficiently. It is helping CIBC Mellon restructure and activate its institutional knowledge so that clients can access the right expertise based on their intent.

Core Challenger Message

CIBC Mellon does not primarily have a content-management problem. It has a client-intent problem. Its expertise is organized and delivered according to the company’s internal structure rather than the immediate needs of institutional visitors. Unless that model changes, a new DXP risks becoming a more efficient way to operate the same experience.

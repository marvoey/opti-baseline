# Progressive Content Types Assessment

An analysis of the 4 core content types used in the Optimizely Demo and how they map to Progressive's internal insurance and contact center jargon.

## 1. Core Principle
* **Will they understand it?** Yes, but it sounds a bit academic.
* **Progressive / Insurance Translation:** **"Base Policy," "National Standard," or "General Guideline."**
* **Demo Advice:** When you introduce this, say: *"This is your Base Policy. If a caller is in a state with no specific legislative quirks, this is the standard answer the consultant provides."*

## 2. Jurisdictional Override
* **Will they understand it?** Yes, and this is actually a very strong, precise term. "Jurisdiction" speaks directly to their Legal/Compliance team, and "Override" speaks to their Architects.
* **Progressive / Insurance Translation:** **"State Exception," "State Variance," or "DOI (Department of Insurance) Rule."**
* **Demo Advice:** Keep this name. It perfectly describes the "Tags as Logic" behavior (that the state rule *overrides* the base rule). Just be sure to mention, *"This handles your State Exceptions automatically..."*

## 3. Procedural Safeguard
* **Will they understand it?** They will understand it implies safety/compliance, but it isn't standard contact center terminology. 
* **Progressive / Insurance Translation:** **"Agent Instructions," "Handling Procedures," "SOPs," or "Next Steps."** (Note: In their RFI, they called this *"workflow designs"*).
* **Demo Advice:** You can keep the name on the UI because it emphasizes risk mitigation, but verbally frame it as: *"This is the Agent SOP or workflow. It tells the consultant exactly what to do in the CRM based on the answer."*

## 4. Statutory Disclosure
* **Will they understand it?** Absolutely. The Legal and Compliance stakeholders will love this term.
* **Progressive / Insurance Translation:** **"Mandatory Script," "Verbatim," "Required Reading," or "Legal Disclaimer."** 
* **Demo Advice:** Call center managers usually just call these "Verbatims" or "Mandatory Scripts." Emphasize the Optimizely lock-down feature here: *"This Statutory Disclosure is your Mandatory Script—because of how we've modeled the content, the AI is literally not allowed to summarize it. It must be read verbatim."*

# CIBC Mellon Content Research

When researching any topic for CIBC Mellon content, follow these rules exactly.

## Source priority

Use sources in this order — prefer the highest-priority source that covers the claim:

1. Official CIBC Mellon website
2. Official CIBC Mellon service pages, thought leadership, reports, and news releases
3. Official BNY sources where relevant to CIBC Mellon's operating model or global network
4. Official CIBC sources where directly relevant
5. Official government, regulatory, tax-authority, central-bank, exchange, depository, or market-operator sources
6. Reputable institutional financial-services sources for general operating-model context

Do not rely on anonymous blogs, generic content farms, unverified summaries, or promotional aggregation sites.

## Accuracy rules

Verify current facts before including them.

Never invent:

- CIBC Mellon products
- Named platforms
- Client results
- Fees
- Performance figures
- Regulatory requirements
- Technology features
- Service availability
- Jurisdictional coverage
- Contractual commitments

When CIBC Mellon's exact service applicability cannot be verified, explain the relevant institutional considerations and use qualified wording (e.g. "typically", "may", "subject to", "where applicable").

# 1. INTRODUCTION

## 1.1 Overview of CIBC Mellon

CIBC Mellon is a Canadian company exclusively focused on the investment servicing needs of Canadian institutional investors and international institutional investors into Canada. Founded in 1996, CIBC Mellon is 50-50 jointly owned by The Bank of New York Mellon (BNY Mellon) and Canadian Imperial Bank of Commerce (CIBC). CIBC Mellon's investment servicing solutions for institutions and corporations are provided in close collaboration with our parent companies, and include custody, multicurrency accounting, fund administration, exchange-traded fund services, recordkeeping, pension services, securities lending services, foreign exchange settlement and treasury services.

As at December 31, 2025, CIBC Mellon had more than C$3.4 trillion of assets under administration. CIBC Mellon is part of the BNY network, which as at December 31, 2025, had US$59.3 trillion in assets under custody and/or administration. CIBC Mellon is a licensed user of the CIBC trade-mark and certain BNY trade-marks, and is the corporate brand of CIBC Mellon Trust Company.

## 1.2 Scope of Work

Through this Request for Proposal ("RFP"), CIBC Mellon is seeking quotes from prospective third parties to deliver a proposal for Website Digital Experience (DXP) and Digital Asset Management (DAM) Platforms services by April 30, 2026.

The Corporate Website is [cibcmellon.com](http://cibcmellon.com/), which was last redesigned in 2015. The look and feel will be provided by CIBC Mellon’s design agency with a strong focus on website accessibility compliance. The scope of this RFP would include collaborating with CIBC Mellon and its design agency to build, develop and support a new website framework on the selected DXP and integrate the DAM platform.

The Prospective Service Provider will provide the following services:

### Digital Experience Platform (DXP)

- Website hosting and deployment, content migration and conversion
- Development services for coding website templates and components as needed
- Centralized content management system (web, mobile, social, etc.)
- Omnichannel delivery and publishing
- Integrated analytics and reporting
- Multilingual and localization support
- Search and navigation optimization
- Workflows, approval management and auditing capabilities
- Client journey mapping
- Integration with CRM and email marketing tools
- User access and role management
- Personalization and user segmentation
- A/B testing and experimentation tools
- API and headless CMS capabilities
- AI powered content suggestions
- Provide best practices for ongoing management of digital experience content and advise on how to continually optimize DXP

### Digital Asset Management (DAM) Platform

- Centralized storage, organization and administration of digital assets (images, videos, documents, etc.)
- Metadata tagging, custom tags and advanced search
- Rights management and usage tracking (if applicable)
- Workflow automation for asset review and approval
- Version control, asset history tracking and audit trail
- Asset usage analytics and reporting
- Secure access permissions and sharing
- Brand consistency and governance
- Bulk upload and batch processing capabilities
- Asset transformation (format conversion, resizing, etc.) with automated jobs (resizing for multiple pre-defined formats)
- Cloud-based accessibility and scalability
- Integration with design and marketing tools (e.g., Adobe, CMS)
- Provide best practices for ongoing management of digital assets and advise on how to continually optimize DAM

Third-Party should describe their understanding of the scope of CIBC Mellon’s requirements as described in Schedule A and demonstrate how the proposed solution and services will meet those requirements.

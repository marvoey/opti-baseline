CIBC MELLON GOVERNANCE & CONTROLS REQUIREMENTS

Supplier Applicability



Scope

The purpose of this document is to provide to all CIBC Mellon suppliers minimum standards which apply to the protection of CIBC Mellon and its Information Assets.

Supplier must design and operate applicable controls to meet or exceed objectives outlined herein.  Where Supplier cannot comply, you must communicate this non-compliance, in writing, to CIBC Mellon and CIBC Mellon must agree with any such changes and/or compensating controls Supplier proposes.

Where Supplier has a contractual obligation to CIBC Mellon which exceeds these requirements, or which more explicitly sets out a requirement in support of these requirements, the contractual obligation governs.

Recordkeeping Migration: Initiation Steps

Multi-employer Canadian pension plans transitioning recordkeeping services to CIBC Mellon must coordinate a structured migration that preserves data integrity for all active members, deferred vested members, and retirees. This workflow covers data transfer initiation, validation, regulatory continuity, and the participant communication plan required to minimize disruption during the transition period.

Prerequisites

  • Incumbent recordkeeper's termination notice submitted in accordance with contract terms
  • Multi-employer plan trust document and most recent plan text available for transfer
  • Plan sponsor or board of trustees resolution authorizing the recordkeeper transition
  • Pension committee or joint board representation identified as the project steering authority
  • Legal counsel engaged to manage data ownership provisions and liability transfer in the transition agreement
  • Participating employer list compiled, including employer codes and contribution remittance details

Process Steps

  1. Submit the Recordkeeping Migration Request to your CIBC Mellon Relationship Manager, providing the plan census count, plan type (MEPP, SEPP, or hybrid DB/DC structure), target migration date, and current recordkeeper contact details for data transfer coordination.
  2. Execute the Transition Services Agreement (TSA) between the plan sponsor, incumbent recordkeeper, and CIBC Mellon. The TSA defines data transfer protocols, liability boundaries, and go-live conditions.
  3. Transfer the plan census data package, including all active members, deferred vested members, retirees, and beneficiaries. Data is submitted in CIBC Mellon's standard data exchange format (CSV or XML schema provided upon engagement).
  4. Complete active member and retiree data validation: CIBC Mellon's Migration team runs automated matching and exception reports against the transferred data. Discrepancies are returned to the incumbent recordkeeper for resolution before proceeding.
  5. Migrate contribution history: Transfer all employee and employer contribution records by member, tax year, and participating employer. CRA-reportable contribution data (pension adjustment, past service pension adjustment, RRSP room impacts) must be reconciled to T4 slips for the prior three tax years.
  6. Set up benefit payment processing: Configure EFT payment instructions for all retirees and beneficiaries receiving regular pension payments. Verify currency, tax withholding elections, and provincial/federal tax coding for each payee. Conduct a parallel payment run for one full cycle before cutover.
  7. Confirm regulatory filing continuity with CIBC Mellon's Compliance team (see regulatory section below).
  8. Develop and distribute the participant communication plan to notify active members and retirees of the service transition, new portal access instructions, and updated contact information.
  9. Execute the go-live cutover on the approved migration date. CIBC Mellon assumes full operational responsibility from this date.

Data Validation and Regulatory Continuity

Data Validation Checklist

  • Member census reconciliation: total count by status (active, deferred, retired, beneficiary) matches incumbent records
  • Contribution history: total contributions per employer and per member reconcile to plan financial statements
  • Benefit payment schedule: all current payees confirmed, payment amounts reconciled to last payroll run
  • Vesting records: service credits and vesting percentages verified for all active and deferred members
  • Enrolment dates and plan entry dates confirmed for plan year calculations
  • Outstanding benefit applications, disability claims, or pending transfers identified and flagged for manual review

Regulatory Filing Continuity

CIBC Mellon coordinates with the plan's actuary, legal counsel, and applicable regulators to ensure no filing deadline lapses during the migration window. Regulatory continuity covers the following obligations: annual information return filing under applicable pension legislation (PBSA, Ontario PBA, or other provincial equivalent); CRA Form T244 (Annual Information Return for Registered Pension Plans); T4A slip preparation and remittance continuity for retirees; and ongoing FSRA or OSFI reporting, as applicable to the plan's jurisdictional registration.

Participant Communication Plan

A standard participant communication package is developed by CIBC Mellon in collaboration with the plan sponsor and includes a transition notice letter (sent 60 days prior to cutover), new portal registration instructions, a member FAQ document, and a dedicated transition helpline for member inquiries. All materials are reviewed and approved by the plan sponsor before distribution. Bilingual (English and French) materials are provided as a standard option for plans with Quebec members.

Expected Timeline

  • TSA execution and data transfer initiation: 4–8 weeks
  • Data validation and exception resolution: 6–12 weeks
  • Parallel payment run and benefit setup: 4–6 weeks (concurrent with validation)
  • Total migration window: 6–12 months from engagement, depending on plan complexity and data quality

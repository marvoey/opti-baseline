Alternative Investment Reporting: Quote Request Process

Asset managers seeking alternative investment fund administration and reporting services for Canadian real estate trusts and other illiquid fund structures can initiate a formal quote request through CIBC Mellon's Alternative Investment Services team. This process captures fund structure, asset inventory, and reporting requirements so that a tailored service proposal and fee schedule can be produced.

Prerequisites

  • Fund structure and legal documentation available (limited partnership agreement, trust deed, or offering memorandum)
  • Asset inventory prepared or in draft, covering fund-of-funds interests, direct real estate holdings, private debt, and other illiquid positions
  • Valuation policy document available, or preliminary agreement on valuation methodology and frequency
  • Current administrator (if any) notified of the review process, or new fund with no incumbent
  • Key investor reporting requirements identified (LP statements, capital account summaries, regulatory returns)

Process Steps

  1. Submit the Alternative Investment Fund Structure Intake Form to your CIBC Mellon Alternative Investment Services Relationship Manager. This form captures fund type, jurisdiction of formation, investor count, asset class breakdown, and reporting cadence.
  2. Provide a current or projected asset inventory, listing each asset by type (direct real estate property, fund-of-funds interest, private equity investment, private debt instrument, or infrastructure asset), fair value basis, and anticipated transaction volume.
  3. Confirm NAV frequency and valuation basis. Real estate funds typically use quarterly NAV with external appraisal; private debt funds may use monthly NAV with internal fair value models. CIBC Mellon's team will advise on best practice for your structure and investor expectations.
  4. Identify investor reporting templates: Specify the required outputs — LP capital account statements, distributable income calculations, K-1 equivalents or T5013 slips (for Canadian partnerships), waterfall distribution reports, and any co-investment reporting obligations.
  5. Define regulatory reporting obligations, including NI 81-106 annual financial statements (if applicable), AIFMD reporting for funds with European investors, and CRA partnership information return (T5013 Summary) requirements.
  6. Request the fee schedule from CIBC Mellon based on the completed intake. The fee schedule will reflect AUM-based administration fees, per-asset reporting fees for complex or illiquid positions, and technology fees for investor portal access.
  7. Review and negotiate the draft Service Level Agreement (SLA), confirming NAV delivery timelines, investor statement issuance deadlines, and escalation procedures for valuation disputes or late data from third-party managers.

Fund Structure Intake and SLA Definition

Asset Inventory Requirements

  • Direct real estate: property address, ownership percentage, appraisal date, most recent appraised value, and income summary
  • Fund-of-funds interests: underlying fund name, manager, most recent available NAV, and distribution history
  • Private debt: borrower, facility type, outstanding principal, interest rate basis, and maturity date
  • Infrastructure or other illiquid assets: asset description, valuation methodology, and supporting documentation

SLA Key Parameters

CIBC Mellon's Alternative Investment SLA defines the following core commitments: NAV calculation delivery timeline (typically 15–30 business days post-quarter-end for real estate; 5–10 business days for private debt); investor statement delivery schedule; turnaround time for audit support packages; and escalation matrix for data quality issues from third-party fund managers or property managers.

Investor Reporting Templates

Standard investor reporting templates are available for download from CIBC Mellon's Alternative Investment Services portal. Custom templates may be developed for funds with specific investor constituency requirements, at an agreed project fee. Template finalization is completed during the onboarding phase and must be approved by the fund's general partner or trustee prior to first distribution.

Expected Timeline

  • Intake form and asset inventory submission: Client-driven; no deadline imposed
  • Quote production following complete intake: 5–10 business days
  • SLA and agreement finalization: 2–4 weeks from quote acceptance
  • First NAV delivery following service commencement: Per agreed fund NAV calendar

Global Compliance Documentation Submission

Foreign institutions establishing a new global institutional relationship with CIBC Mellon must submit a comprehensive compliance documentation package before any services are activated. This workflow covers jurisdiction-specific checklists, tax self-certification forms, beneficial owner declarations, and the required compliance officer attestations that govern the relationship from inception.

Prerequisites

  • Engagement letter or signed RFP response from CIBC Mellon Relationship Management confirming intent to proceed
  • Compliance officer or equivalent authorized representative identified who can execute self-certification forms
  • Tax residency documentation available for each legal entity in scope
  • Beneficial owner information compiled for all natural persons holding qualifying ownership interests
  • Legal counsel available to advise on jurisdictional self-certification requirements if the entity spans multiple tax residency jurisdictions

Process Steps

  1. Receive the jurisdiction-specific compliance checklist from CIBC Mellon's Compliance Onboarding team. This checklist is tailored to your entity type, domicile jurisdiction, and FATF risk classification, and specifies which forms and certifications are required for your particular structure.
  2. Complete the FATCA self-certification form (W-8BEN-E) for entities claiming non-US status. Select the appropriate chapter 3 and chapter 4 entity classification, confirm treaty eligibility where applicable, and have the form executed by a duly authorized representative. Submit via the CIBC Mellon secure document portal.
  3. Complete the CRS Self-Certification Form in accordance with the OECD Common Reporting Standard. Identify all jurisdictions of tax residency and provide Tax Identification Numbers (TINs) for each. Entities with complex multi-jurisdictional residency positions should obtain legal advice before completing this form.
  4. Provide tax residency documentation: Submit official certificates of tax residency issued by the relevant national tax authority for each jurisdiction declared on the CRS form, or an equivalent legal opinion where a certificate is not available.
  5. Submit the Beneficial Owner Declaration using the CIBC Mellon template. Declare all natural persons who, directly or indirectly, own or control 25% or more of the entity (or 10% for higher-risk classifications). For entities with no natural person beneficial owners above the threshold, provide a senior managing official declaration.
  6. Submit the Sanctions Screening Attestation, signed by an authorized officer, confirming that the entity and its beneficial owners are not subject to sanctions designations under OFAC, UN, EU, or Canadian (OSFI) lists, and that no sanctions-designated persons have authority over the entity's accounts.
  7. Obtain compliance officer sign-off on the completed documentation package. Your Chief Compliance Officer (or equivalent) must countersign the compliance certification cover sheet confirming the accuracy and completeness of all submitted documentation.
  8. CIBC Mellon Compliance reviews the package and issues a determination within 15 business days of receiving a complete submission. Incomplete packages are returned with a deficiency notice specifying outstanding items.

Jurisdiction-Specific Requirements

FATCA and W-8BEN-E Guidance

Foreign financial institutions (FFIs) must confirm their FATF GIIN (Global Intermediary Identification Number) on the W-8BEN-E if registered with the IRS under a Model 1 or Model 2 IGA. Non-participating FFIs and entities from non-IGA jurisdictions are subject to 30% US withholding on US-source payments. CIBC Mellon's Compliance team can advise on the appropriate classification but does not provide tax advice; consult your tax counsel for classification decisions.

CRS Self-Certification Requirements

  • All entities must declare tax residency in each jurisdiction where they are treated as resident for tax purposes
  • Financial institutions must identify their CRS institution type (Reporting FI, Non-Reporting FI, Active NFE, or Passive NFE)
  • Passive NFEs must additionally disclose controlling persons and their tax residencies
  • Recertification is required if any material change in tax residency or entity classification occurs

Accepted Document Formats and Submission

  • All documents must be submitted as PDF via CIBC Mellon's encrypted document portal
  • Self-certification forms must be executed with wet signature or advanced electronic signature (AES) compliant with eIDAS or equivalent local standard
  • Documents in languages other than English or French must be accompanied by a certified translation
  • Notarized or apostilled copies required for constitutional documents from jurisdictions where originals cannot be provided

What Happens Next

Upon compliance approval, CIBC Mellon's Onboarding team coordinates with your Relationship Manager to proceed with account opening and service activation. Any ongoing compliance monitoring obligations — such as periodic re-certification, material change notification, or annual sanctions screening — are set out in the client services agreement executed at onboarding. Compliance documentation must be refreshed on a three-year cycle or upon any material change in entity structure, ownership, or tax residency.

Opening a Canadian Sub-Custody Account

Foreign institutions and US-based banks seeking to hold Canadian securities through CIBC Mellon must complete a formal sub-custody account opening process. This workflow covers eligibility, documentation, and enrollment with Canada's central securities depository.

Eligibility Requirements

Before initiating account opening, confirm your institution meets the following baseline criteria:

  • Regulated financial institution or bank holding company domiciled outside Canada
  • Active Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) issued through a GLEIF-accredited Local Operating Unit
  • No active sanctions designations under Canadian, US, EU, or UN screening lists
  • Counterparty due diligence complete or initiated with CIBC Mellon Relationship Management

Process Steps

  1. Contact your CIBC Mellon Relationship Manager to initiate the sub-custody account request and receive the onboarding package.
  2. Complete the Entity Due Diligence (EDD) intake form, including ultimate beneficial owner (UBO) disclosure and ownership structure chart.
  3. Submit all required AML/KYC documentation to CIBC Mellon's Compliance team (see documentation checklist below).
  4. Receive and execute the Sub-Custody Agreement, including schedule of fees, service levels, and settlement instruction terms.
  5. Provide standing settlement instructions (SSIs) for the account, including correspondent bank details and SWIFT BIC codes.
  6. Coordinate CDSX participant enrollment with CDS Clearing and Depository Services if direct participant access is required.
  7. Complete testing and reconciliation validation with CIBC Mellon Operations prior to go-live.

Required Documentation and Timeline

Required Documents

  • Certified copy of Certificate of Incorporation or equivalent registration document
  • Board resolution authorizing the sub-custody relationship and naming authorized signatories
  • Specimen signatures for all authorized signatories
  • LEI confirmation (printout from GLEIF registry)
  • Most recent annual report or audited financial statements
  • Regulatory license or charter confirmation from home-country regulator
  • AML policy summary or certification from Chief Compliance Officer
  • FATCA/CRS self-certification forms (W-8BEN-E or equivalent)

CDSX Participant Enrollment

If your institution requires direct CDSX participant status (as opposed to accessing the depository through CIBC Mellon as your agent), a separate CDS membership application is required. CIBC Mellon's Operations team will coordinate this process with CDS on your behalf and advise on participant classification (direct or indirect).

Expected Timeline

  • AML/KYC review: 10–15 business days from complete document submission
  • Agreement execution and SSI setup: 5–10 business days
  • CDSX enrollment (if applicable): Additional 15–20 business days
  • Total estimated onboarding timeline: 30–45 business days from complete documentation receipt

What Happens Next

Once your account is active, CIBC Mellon will provide access to the client portal for transaction monitoring, position reporting, and settlement instruction management. Your dedicated Relationship Manager remains your primary point of contact for service inquiries throughout the relationship.

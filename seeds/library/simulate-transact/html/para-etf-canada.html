Canadian ETF Launch Checklist

Asset managers launching a new Canadian ETF must coordinate several concurrent workstreams before trading commences. CIBC Mellon provides an integrated onboarding process covering custody, fund administration, transfer agency, and regulatory coordination. Use this checklist to sequence your launch activities correctly.

Prerequisites

  • Prospectus filed or in active preparation with applicable provincial securities regulator (CSA/provincial OSC, AMF, or BCSC)
  • Lead portfolio management team and sub-adviser agreements finalized
  • ETF structure confirmed (physical replication, synthetic, actively managed, or alternative)
  • Fund sponsor legal entity registered and in good standing under NI 81-102 or NI 81-104
  • Designated Broker agreement executed or in negotiation with at least one TSX-registered market maker

Process Steps

  1. Submit the CIBC Mellon ETF Onboarding Request Form to your Relationship Manager, specifying fund type, expected AUM at launch, and target launch date.
  2. Complete prospectus readiness review with CIBC Mellon's Fund Administration team to confirm that fund facts documents and investment objectives are aligned with NAV calculation methodology.
  3. Confirm CDS eligibility for the ETF units by coordinating with CDS Clearing and Depository Services to obtain a CUSIP and ensure CDS book-entry eligibility.
  4. Confirm TSX listing eligibility and submit the TSX listing application. CIBC Mellon Operations will coordinate CDSX participant setup to support creation/redemption flows.
  5. Configure NAV and iNAV services: Agree on NAV calculation frequency (daily, end of day), iNAV dissemination frequency (real-time or 15-second intervals), and pricing sources for underlying securities.
  6. Enroll with CIBC Mellon Transfer Agency to establish unitholder registry, creation/redemption processing, and DTC/CDS settlement linkages for Designated Broker flows.
  7. Coordinate regulatory filing for the final prospectus receipt, SEDAR+ submission, and any applicable NI 81-106 continuous disclosure obligations.
  8. Complete parallel-run testing of NAV calculations, creation/redemption settlement, and transfer agency registry updates prior to launch.

Service Setup and Regulatory Coordination

NAV and iNAV Configuration

CIBC Mellon's Fund Administration team will configure the NAV engine based on your fund's investment policy statement and approved pricing hierarchy. For fixed income or alternative ETFs, a bespoke pricing waterfall may be required. iNAV dissemination is coordinated through an approved iNAV calculation agent and transmitted to TSX for real-time display.

Transfer Agency Enrollment

  • Provide fund legal name, CUSIP, and ticker symbol once TSX listing is confirmed
  • Submit Designated Broker agreement and authorized creation/redemption basket instructions
  • Confirm settlement cycle (T+1 for Canadian equities; T+2 may apply for international underlying assets)
  • Establish anti-dilution levy or swing pricing policy documentation, if applicable

Regulatory Filing Coordination

CIBC Mellon coordinates with your legal counsel and SEDAR+ filing agent to ensure the preliminary prospectus, amendment, and final receipt are aligned with the fund's operational readiness. NI 81-106 ongoing disclosure obligations, including management reports of fund performance (MRFPs) and annual financial statements, are included in the service scope from launch.

Expected Timeline

  • Onboarding and documentation: 4–6 weeks from form submission
  • CDS/TSX eligibility and listing: 4–8 weeks (concurrent with prospectus review)
  • Parallel-run testing: 2–3 weeks
  • Target total launch window: 10–16 weeks from initial engagement

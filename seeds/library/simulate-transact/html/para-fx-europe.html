Initiating an FX Execution Workflow

European corporate sponsors executing foreign exchange transactions through CIBC Mellon must follow a structured pre-trade authorization and submission workflow. This process ensures compliance with MiFID II best execution obligations and establishes the settlement instruction chain required for timely settlement.

Pre-Trade Authorization Requirements

Before submitting a trade ticket, confirm the following are in place:

  • Active FX trading authorization on file with CIBC Mellon, signed by an authorized officer
  • Approved FX limit schedule (notional, tenor, and currency pair restrictions)
  • LEI registered and validated with a recognized trade repository
  • MiFID II client categorization confirmed (Professional Client or Eligible Counterparty)
  • EMIR reporting delegation agreement executed, if CIBC Mellon reports on your behalf

Process Steps

  1. Confirm FX limit availability with your CIBC Mellon FX Desk prior to trade initiation. Limits are checked in real time against your approved schedule.
  2. Agree on indicative rate and tenor with your FX Dealer, specifying value date, currency pair, and notional amount.
  3. Submit the trade ticket via the CIBC Mellon FX portal, SWIFT, or encrypted email, depending on your agreed channel. Include your authorization reference number.
  4. Receive trade confirmation from CIBC Mellon's FX Operations team. Review and affirm within the confirmation window (same day for spot, T+1 for forward trades).
  5. Verify settlement instructions already on file or provide updated SSIs for the currency pair. SSI changes require 24-hour notice and authorized signatory confirmation.
  6. Receive MiFID II best execution disclosure documentation following trade execution. Review and retain for regulatory recordkeeping purposes.
  7. Monitor settlement via the client portal. Flag any settlement fails to CIBC Mellon Operations by the currency's standard cut-off time.

Authorization, Submission, and Settlement

FX Limit and Tenor Confirmation

All FX transactions are subject to the limits documented in your signed FX Authorization Agreement. Trades exceeding established notional limits or extending beyond approved tenors require prior written approval from your CIBC Mellon Relationship Manager and may require credit committee review.

Settlement Instruction Setup

  • Standing Settlement Instructions (SSIs) must be registered in CIBC Mellon's SSI database for each currency pair
  • New SSIs require authorized signatory sign-off and a 24-business-hour validation period
  • SSI amendments must be submitted via authenticated channel (portal or authenticated SWIFT message)
  • CIBC Mellon will confirm SSI receipt and activation in writing before the SSI becomes effective

Post-Trade Reporting

Under EMIR and MiFID II, all over-the-counter FX derivatives (forwards, options) are subject to trade reporting obligations. If CIBC Mellon reports on your behalf under a delegation agreement, you remain responsible for ensuring your LEI is active and the reported data is accurate. Spot FX transactions are not reportable under EMIR but remain subject to MiFID II best execution documentation requirements.

What Happens Next

Executed trades are reflected in your position and cash reporting by end of business on trade date. Settlement confirmations are issued on value date. Contact your CIBC Mellon FX Operations representative for any post-trade queries or settlement exceptions.

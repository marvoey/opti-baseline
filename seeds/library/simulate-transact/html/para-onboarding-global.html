Global AML/KYC Onboarding Requirements

Foreign institutions initiating a global custody or fund services relationship with CIBC Mellon must complete a comprehensive AML/KYC review before any accounts are opened or services activated. This review is conducted by CIBC Mellon's Financial Crime Compliance team and follows standards aligned with FATF Recommendations, Canadian PCMLTFA requirements, and applicable local regulations in each jurisdiction of operation.

Prerequisites

  • Formal engagement letter or request for proposal submitted to CIBC Mellon Relationship Management
  • Preliminary sanctions screening completed (CIBC Mellon conducts this internally upon receiving entity name and LEI)
  • Ultimate beneficial owner (UBO) information available for all owners holding 25% or greater interest (10% for higher-risk jurisdictions)
  • Authorized compliance officer identified who can certify submitted documentation
  • Legal counsel available to provide certified translations if original documents are not in English or French

Process Steps

  1. Submit your entity onboarding request to your CIBC Mellon Relationship Manager. You will receive a secure document portal link and a jurisdiction-specific documentation checklist within three business days.
  2. Complete the Entity Due Diligence (EDD) intake form. This covers corporate structure, ownership chart, nature of business, source of funds, and expected transaction volumes and types.
  3. Disclose all Ultimate Beneficial Owners (UBOs) in accordance with FATF Recommendation 24. For complex structures (trusts, funds of funds, sovereign vehicles), a layer-by-layer ownership chart is required.
  4. Submit all required documentation to the secure portal (see document list below). Do not submit via unencrypted email.
  5. CIBC Mellon Compliance conducts sanctions screening against OFAC, UN, EU, and Canadian (OSFI) lists. Adverse media and PEP screening is conducted simultaneously.
  6. Undergo FATF jurisdiction classification review. Institutions from FATF grey-listed or non-cooperative jurisdictions are subject to Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) and senior management sign-off.
  7. Receive the compliance determination from CIBC Mellon's Financial Crime Compliance team. Standard reviews are completed within 15 business days of complete document receipt; EDD reviews may take 30–45 business days.
  8. Respond to any clarification requests within five business days to avoid delays. Unresolved queries beyond 20 business days may require re-initiation of the review.

Document Submission and Review Process

Required Documents

  • Certified copy of certificate of incorporation, charter, or equivalent legal formation document
  • Certified register of directors and officers (dated within 12 months)
  • Certified register of shareholders or members (confirming UBO chain)
  • AML/KYC policy and procedure summary, certified by the Chief Compliance Officer
  • Most recent audited financial statements (within 24 months)
  • Regulatory license or authorization from the home-country financial regulator
  • FATCA/CRS self-certification: W-8BEN-E (for US tax purposes) and CRS Self-Certification form
  • Beneficial owner declaration form (CIBC Mellon template)
  • Sanctions screening attestation signed by an authorized officer
  • Passports or government-issued ID for directors and UBOs (where required by risk rating)

Accepted Document Formats

CIBC Mellon accepts certified copies (notarized or apostilled as applicable), original documents, or certified true copies signed by a regulated professional (lawyer, notary, accountant). Digital uploads are accepted through the secure document portal in PDF format. Documents in languages other than English or French must be accompanied by a certified translation.

Escalation Path for Complex Structures

Entities with non-standard ownership structures — including sovereign wealth funds, charitable foundations, blind trusts, or structures involving multiple holding layers — are escalated to CIBC Mellon's Enhanced Due Diligence committee. Your Relationship Manager will advise on the additional documentation required and the extended review timeline. CIBC Mellon reserves the right to decline onboarding where compliance obligations cannot be satisfied.

OSFI Capital Adequacy Framework

The Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI) administers Canada's principal capital adequacy regime for federally regulated insurance companies. All federally regulated life and health insurers must comply with the Life Insurance Capital Adequacy Test (LICAT), which replaced the Minimum Continuing Capital and Surplus Requirements (MCCSR) framework effective January 1, 2018. LICAT establishes a risk-sensitive, factor-based capital measurement system that aggregates credit risk, market risk, insurance risk, and operational risk into a Total Ratio. OSFI's supervisory target ratio is 100%, with a Company Action Level set at 90%, below which regulatory intervention is triggered. Insurers must maintain capital adequate to meet these thresholds on both a consolidated and solo-entity basis.

For property and casualty (P&C) insurers, the analogous framework is the Minimum Capital Test (MCT). The MCT measures available capital against required capital derived from specified risk factors applied to assets and liabilities. OSFI's supervisory target is 150%, with a Company Action Level at 100%. The MCT was substantially revised in its January 2023 iteration to strengthen treatment of interest rate risk and reinsurance recoverable credit risk. Both LICAT and MCT require filing with OSFI on a quarterly basis (within 45 days of quarter-end) and on an annual basis (within 60 days of fiscal year-end) for federally regulated insurers.

Key Regulatory Guidelines and Requirements

Guideline B-3: Sound Reinsurance Practices and Procedures

OSFI Guideline B-3 sets out expectations for the governance, risk management, and operational controls surrounding a federally regulated insurer's reinsurance program. Key requirements include:

  • Board-approved reinsurance strategy that articulates risk appetite and maximum retention limits by line of business
  • Counterparty due diligence and ongoing monitoring of reinsurer financial strength ratings
  • Clear contractual terms addressing claims settlement, offset rights, and commutation provisions
  • Maintenance of adequate documentation for all treaties, facultative placements, and finite reinsurance arrangements
  • Annual review and attestation by the Appointed Actuary confirming the reinsurance program's adequacy

OSFI expects that no single unauthorized reinsurer accounts for a disproportionate share of total ceded exposure. Where concentration risks are elevated, additional capital loads may be applied within the LICAT or MCT calculation.

Guideline E-23: Model Risk Management

OSFI Guideline E-23, issued in May 2017 and updated under the 2023 Supervisory Framework review, establishes comprehensive expectations for the identification, measurement, and governance of model risk across federally regulated financial institutions, including insurers. Under E-23, insurers are required to:

  • Maintain a complete inventory of all models used in capital calculation, pricing, reserving, and asset-liability management
  • Conduct independent model validation for material models prior to first use and at prescribed intervals thereafter
  • Establish a model governance committee or equivalent oversight body with clearly defined roles and escalation procedures
  • Implement challenger model testing and back-testing processes to assess ongoing model performance
  • Document model limitations, assumptions, and compensating controls where known model weaknesses exist

Insurers relying on internal models for LICAT capital calculation are subject to heightened scrutiny under E-23. OSFI may conduct targeted model reviews as part of its Supervisory Review and Evaluation Process (SREP), and significant model deficiencies may result in capital add-ons imposed under OSFI's supervisory authority.

Implementation and Reporting Timeline

  • Quarterly LICAT/MCT Filing: Within 45 days of each quarter-end (Q1–Q3)
  • Annual LICAT/MCT Filing: Within 60 days of fiscal year-end
  • Appointed Actuary Annual Report: Filed concurrently with annual financial statements
  • Model Inventory Review: Minimum annually, with material model changes triggering immediate notification to OSFI

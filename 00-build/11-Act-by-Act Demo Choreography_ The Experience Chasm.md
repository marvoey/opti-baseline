## Act 3: The Differentiating Reveal — The Single-Template Permutation Engine

### Goal
Prove that Optimizely eliminates the 5,760-page problem by shifting from *static page creation* to *dynamic component assembly* with built-in governance guardrails.

---

### Beat 1: The Paradigm Shift — "What is a Content Block?"
* **Concept:** Define content blocks vs. monolithic pages in plain business English.
* **The Analogy:** The *LEGO brick with a smart luggage tag*.

#### Challenger Script:
> *"Up until now, the digital industry has forced marketing to think in **Pages**. A page is a monolithic slab of concrete: headline, body copy, case study, and form all glued together under one fixed URL. If you want to change one sentence for Aris Thorne at Unilever Foods, you have to clone the entire slab.
> 
> Optimizely breaks that slab into **Content Blocks**.
> 
> Think of a content block as an independent LEGO brick that has two things:
> 1. **One specific message:** A single headline, a single case study, or a single meeting button.
> 2. **A smart label (taxonomy tag):** A metadata tag that says: *'I belong to E-commerce'* or *'I belong to Packaged Foods'*.
> 
> You don't build pages anymore. Your copywriters author individual blocks once. Then, when a visitor arrives, our engine snaps the matching blocks together on the fly into a single layout."*

Use Optimizely Content Graph (GraphQL) to instantly query the entire repository for products or pages where seoDescription is null.

2 The View

Surface these results in a custom dashboard right inside the Optimizely UI using a custom gadget, or export them to your external SEO tool.

Editors click directly from the dashboard report into the specific CMS page to update the metadata immediately.

Our demo objective

Our Demo Objective

Bring to life how your platform enables Liberty Mutual to create, govern, reuse, discover, personalize, and deliver trusted content across customer and agent experiences.

To achieve this goal, we seek to:

  • ✓See hands-on demonstrations of Liberty Mutual’s priority CMS / DXP use cases, using realistic insurance content, audiences, and workflows.
  • ✓Understand how marketing and content teams can independently create, preview, approve, publish, and optimize experiences while preserving legal, accessibility, brand, and compliance controls.
  • ✓Evaluate how the platform manages structured, reusable content that can be distributed consistently across LM.com, the Independent Agent Portal, search, AI experiences, and other digital channels.
  • ✓Explore how the platform supports search, AI discoverability, personalization, analytics, APIs, and integration with Liberty Mutual’s existing ecosystem.
  • ✓Understand how the platform will scale and evolve alongside Liberty Mutual, including its product roadmap, implementation approach, composable architecture, and support for emerging AI-enabled experiences.
Five outcomes Liberty Mutual wants from the demo: real use cases, self-service with controls, structured reusable content, search/AI/personalization/integration, and a credible roadmap.

Fragmented content management

Content is managed across multiple platforms and operating models, creating duplication, inconsistent experiences, and unclear sources of truth.

LibertyMutual.com use case overview

Trusted Answers, Wherever the Journey Begins

Demo narrative

A prospective customer asks a natural-language insurance question. The platform retrieves approved, structured Liberty Mutual content, provides an understandable answer, cites or links to the authoritative source, and presents a useful next step. At the same time, Liberty Mutual’s publishing team can create and update the underlying content through governed self-service tools.

Outcome

Once approved and published, that content becomes available to web pages, site search, enterprise search, AI answer engines, and downstream apps without separate manual publishing processes.

Independent Agent (IA) Portal use case overview

One Relevant Liberty Experience

Demo narrative

An independent agent signs in to the IA Portal and lands on a dashboard of product and bundling content, tailored to their role, state, and agency and aligned to current Liberty business priorities. A Liberty marketer changes which products are emphasized, and the agent's view updates without development work. Every portal page shares one brand and design because pages are built from governed templates and components. A content administrator then updates an approved disclosure once, and it appears in the IA Portal and on LM.com.

Outcome

The agent sees what to focus on and finds approved content to act on a client opportunity, without searching or waiting for IT.

How we will decide

Evaluation Criteria

We will be gauging which platform/supplier best accomplishes the demo objective of…

“Bring to life how your platform accomplishes the objectives of the Liberty Mutual team as we select our Content Management Solution.”

Specifically, we are looking for a supplier who...

  • ✓Demonstrates depth & experience within the Insurance industry
  • ✓Brings our use cases to life using industry relevant data and our sources/destinations where possible
  • ✓Is investing in platform growth over the next 2-3 years alongside Liberty Mutual
  • ✓That makes trusted content AI-ready by design so Liberty can remain the authoritative source wherever customers seek answers.
Four criteria: insurance depth, real data and sources, 2–3 year investment, and AI-ready trusted content.

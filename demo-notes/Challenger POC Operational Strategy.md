To deliver a high-impact **Challenger-style POC** on June 25, you must pivot the conversation away from "building a website" and toward **"solving the operational friction of C$3.4 trillion in assets."**

The Challenger approach requires you to **teach, tailor, and take control.** You aren't just showing features; you are showing them how to survive the "Information Velocity Squeeze" of 2026.

Here is a structured POC plan for your June 25 on-site delivery:

---

### 1. The Opening: "The Reframe" (Teaching for Differentiation)
**Don’t start with a slide about Optimizely.** Start with a slide about **CIBC Mellon's current "Cost of Inaction."**
*   **The Message:** "Your 2015 website isn't just a branding issue; it's a regulatory and operational bottleneck. In a T+1 settlement world, every second your team spends manually tagging a Private Equity PDF or waiting for a French translation is a direct risk to your SLAs."
*   **The Challenger Insight:** Move them from seeing the project as *Marketing* to seeing it as *Infrastructure*.

---

### 2. The POC Scenario A: The "T+1 Emergency" (Addressing Velocity)
**The Requirement (from Schedule D):** Content publishing scheduling, Personalization, Omnichannel.
**The Challenger Demo:**
*   **The Setup:** A sudden regulatory change in Canada requires an immediate update to all Pension Fund clients regarding settlement procedures.
*   **The Demo:** 
    1.  Use **Opal AI** to take a raw regulatory brief and instantly generate a client-facing update.
    2.  Show **Personalization** by filtering the update so it *only* appears on the dashboard for "Pension Fund Managers," while "Retail Asset Managers" see their normal view.
    3.  **The "Kicker":** Show the **Omnichannel** push. One click sends this to the website, the mobile portal, and the secure client email tool.
*   **The Challenger Point:** "We just saved your team 4 hours of manual coordination. In a T+1 world, those 4 hours are the difference between a successful settlement and a multi-million dollar fail."

---

### 3. The POC Scenario B: The "Private Market Asset Intake" (Addressing Dark Data)
**The Requirement (from Schedule D):** DAM, Metadata tagging, AI automation.
**The Challenger Demo:**
*   **The Setup:** A client sends a complex 50-page "Capital Call Notice" for a Real Estate fund. This is usually "Dark Data" that someone has to read and manually tag.
*   **The Demo:**
    1.  Upload the raw PDF into the **Optimizely DAM**.
    2.  Use **Opal AI** to "read" the document and automatically extract metadata (Fund Name, Amount, Due Date, Jurisdiction).
    3.  Show how this asset is now **instantly searchable** by anyone in the firm without a single human keystroke.
*   **The Challenger Point:** "Industry data says your teams waste 29% of their time on non-essential tasks. We are reclaiming that 29% by turning your DAM from a 'storage closet' into an 'Intelligence Engine.'"

---

### 4. The POC Scenario C: The "OSFI Compliance Moat" (Addressing Resilience)
**The Requirement (from Schedule D/B):** Audit trail, Versioning, User attestation, SSO.
**The Challenger Demo:**
*   **The Setup:** An OSFI auditor walks in and asks for a history of who approved a specific piece of ESG content three months ago.
*   **The Demo:**
    1.  Navigate to the **Audit Trail** for a specific content block.
    2.  Show the exact **Workflow** history: who edited, who approved (the "Second Set of Eyes" principle), and when it went live.
    3.  Export the **User Attestation Report** (Schedule D Requirement) showing exactly who has access to the CMS and when they last logged in.
*   **The Challenger Point:** "Agility is useless if you can't prove it's compliant. We aren't just giving you a CMS; we're giving you a 'Compliance Fortress' that keeps the regulators happy while your marketing team moves at the speed of light."

---

### 5. The Closing: "The Commercial Outcome"
**Don't end on "any questions?"** End on the **Business Case.**
*   **The Wrap-up:** "June 25 is the start of shifting your team from 'Admins' to 'Advisors.' By automating the 29% of wasted time through Opal and the DAM, you aren't just updating a website—you are scaling your ability to service C$3.4 trillion without adding headcount."

### Tactical Tips for June 25:
*   **Use Their Language:** Mention "OSFI B-10," "T+1," and "Pension Portfolios" throughout.
*   **Live Translation:** Demonstrate Opal translating a "Thought Leadership" piece into French (Schedule D Requirement) live in the editor. It's a huge pain point in Canada that creates instant "wow" factor.
*   **Mobile Preview:** Show the "Mobile Friendly View" (Schedule D) on your phone or tablet during the meeting. Most institutional sites fail here.

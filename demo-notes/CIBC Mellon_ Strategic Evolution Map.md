# CIBC Mellon DXP/DAM: Strategic Evolution Map
## From Core Requirements to Commercial Insights (June 25 POC)

To execute a flawless **Challenger Pitch** on June 25, we must demonstrate that we completely fulfill their explicit RFP requirements, while systematically "building a ladder" to a much higher-stakes operational conversation. 

This map outlines the exact evolution from what they *asked for* to what they *actually need* to solve their business friction.

---

1.  **The "Acknowledge" (Ground Floor):** "You asked us for a redesigned website, a secure file library, and basic personalization. We have checked all of those boxes as standard, pre-built features."
2.  **The "Disrupt" (Step Up):** "But as Canada’s leader in asset servicing, your business doesn't operate in a standard environment. T+1 has compressed your margins of error to zero. Alternatives are burying your teams in unstructured PDFs. OSFI B-10 is tightening the screws on your tech stack."
3.  **The "Reframe" (The Top Floor):** "That is why we didn't just build a website. We evolved your requirements into an **Operational Command Center**. We are showing you a DXP that behaves like a market radar, a DAM that reads your documents for you, and a GenUI engine that handles compliance on autopilot."
